# Article 1 -- Purpose

TrumpMaster is a strategic trick-taking card game inspired by Callbreak
while establishing its own competitive identity.

It rewards: - Observation - Memory - Planning - Deduction - Calculated
risk

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article 2 -- Vision

TrumpMaster shall be: - Easy to learn - Difficult to master - Fair -
Deterministic - Competitive - Replayable

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article 3 -- Core Philosophy

1.  Gameplay before graphics.
2.  Strategy before automation.
3.  Skill before luck.
4.  Information must be earned.
5.  Equal rules for every player.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article 4 -- Design Principles

-   One authoritative Rule Engine.
-   UI never changes gameplay.
-   AI obeys the same rules as humans.
-   Hidden information stays hidden during live play.
-   Every replay must reproduce the original match.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article 5 -- Official Terminology

  Term         Definition
  ------------ -----------------------------------
  Game         Eight deals
  Deal         Distribution of all 52 cards
  Trick        One set of four played cards
  Turn         Opportunity for one player to act
  Dealer       Player dealing cards
  Leader       Player leading a trick
  Trump Suit   Suit of dealer's last dealt card
  Lead Suit    Suit of first card in a trick

The term **Round** is not used in official documentation.

# Article 6 -- Table Layout

  Seat     Position

# Article 7 -- Official Turn Order

Seat0 → Seat1 → Seat2 → Seat3 → Seat0

This governs dealing, bidding, card play and dealer rotation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article 8 -- Game Structure

Game

→ 8 Deals

→ 13 Tricks per Deal

→ 4 Cards per Trick

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Article 9 -- Constitution Governance

Every gameplay change must:

1.  Be discussed.
2.  Be documented.
3.  Receive a ruleset version.
4.  Be reflected in the Rule Verification Matrix.
5.  Be implemented only after approval.
